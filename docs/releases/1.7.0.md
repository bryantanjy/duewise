# Duewise 1.7.0

- Share active or archived reminder summaries through the native Android/iOS share sheet.
- Includes name, formatted and ISO due date, repeat schedule, optional cost, and archived status. Notes, attachments, history, and internal IDs are excluded.
- Shared text is a snapshot and does not create or sync a reminder on the recipient device.
- Opening, cancelling, or completing sharing does not modify the reminder. Errors appear on the details screen; no delivery confirmation is claimed.
- iPad share sheets anchor to the share control. Build numbers continue to auto-increment through EAS.

Physical checks: share a cost-bearing reminder, cancel and retry, share an archived reminder, confirm notes/documents are absent, verify the iPad popover, and test large text and dark mode.
