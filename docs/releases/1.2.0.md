# Duewise 1.2.0

Prepared November 10, 2026. Minor release for added functionality; existing reminder records remain compatible.

## Features

- Reminder templates for passports, insurance, subscriptions, warranties, and birthdays.
- Android and iOS home-screen widgets, including the corrected Android home-screen discovery flag.
- Snooze choices: one hour, tomorrow, next week, or a custom date and time. Tomorrow and next week use the reminder's selected alert time.
- Tomorrow action on notifications, alongside Snooze 1 hour and Mark done. The OS controls how many actions are initially visible.

Snoozes add temporary alerts; due dates, regular alerts, and repeat rules are preserved. Completion/archive removes outstanding snoozes. Temporary snoozes remain excluded from backups.

## Versioning and build

The app config, package manifest, package lock and Settings fallback use 1.2.0. EAS uses remote build numbers; development, preview and production profiles have automatic increments enabled. A new build has not been submitted by this change.

Build an installable Android APK using `npx eas-cli@latest build --platform android --profile preview`. Local builds require the Android SDK. Widget native changes require a new binary, not an OTA update.

## Device verification

1. Open a reminder and choose Snooze. Check all four choices and the displayed scheduled time.
2. Confirm tomorrow/next week use the reminder's chosen time, including near month/year boundaries.
3. Choose a custom future date/time; reject past or elapsed selections. Cancel without scheduling an alert.
4. Verify the extra alert in the schedule and confirm the due date and regular alerts remain unchanged.
5. Complete/archive the reminder and confirm outstanding snoozes are cancelled.
6. Test Tomorrow from a notification with the app foregrounded, backgrounded and closed. Verify duplicate response delivery adds only one alert.
7. Verify disabled notification permission/channel reports a failure without claiming success.
8. Confirm Settings reports 1.2.0 and retest the widget on the new APK.

Automated tests and JavaScript exports do not replace native device verification. Android notification delivery may be delayed by the OS.
